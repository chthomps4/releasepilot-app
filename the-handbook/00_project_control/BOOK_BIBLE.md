# The Handbook — Book Bible

## Project identity

**Full working title:** The Forbidden Knowledge Bible: A Complete Codex of Ancient Knowledge, Lost Etymology, Cosmic Systems, Forgotten Technologies, Hidden Source Trails, and Modern Science Bridges

**Short title:** The Handbook

**Core promise:** preserve how to think, not only what to know.

## Tone

The tone should be serious, durable, source-aware, grounded, and imaginative only when clearly labeled as interpretation or speculation.

Preferred framing:

- continuity archive
- source-trail guide
- cultural memory map
- research manual
- claim-verification handbook

Avoid:

- fear-based apocalyptic framing
- unsupported hidden-elite claims
- treating ancient symbolism as modern science
- conspiracy certainty
- flattening all cultures into one universal code
- overstating weak evidence

## Depth Control System

Every major topic should be organized into reader-selectable knowledge layers.

### Level 1 — Core Facts / Well-Supported

Primary sources, peer-reviewed scholarship, archaeology, inscriptions, artifact records, museum/catalogue data, reliable editions, and cautious consensus.

### Level 2 — Interpretation / Scholarly and Contextual

Reasonable scholarly interpretation, contextual reconstruction, debated but plausible readings, and careful synthesis.

### Level 3 — Connections / Comparative

Cross-cultural comparisons, recurring motifs, symbolic parallels, structural similarities, and historical echoes. These are comparisons, not proof.

### Level 4 — Speculative Frontier

Controlled exploration of debated, fringe, or highly uncertain possibilities. Always labeled speculative.

### Level 5 — Unsupported / Myth / Modern Invention

Claims that are unsupported, misquoted, invented, exaggerated, or not yet evidenced. This level should correct the record, not mock curiosity.

## Permanent research lanes

1. Pre-Egypt / Pre-Christian Deep Antiquity
2. Ancient language and etymology
3. Lost, suppressed, damaged, or hard-to-access textual traditions
4. Ancient practical knowledge and technologies
5. Astronomical and cosmological systems
6. Symbol, ritual, architecture, and memory systems
7. Modern science bridges with strict guardrails
8. Source trail methodology
9. Claim auditing and misinformation correction

## Modern science guardrail

Modern topics such as James Webb, Euclid, Roman Space Telescope, gravitational-wave observatories, and quantum computing may be included only as modern bridges. Ancient systems may be compared to modern questions, but they must not be presented as if ancient people possessed modern instrumental physics without evidence.

## Chapter operating rules

Each chapter should include:

- chapter thesis
- core definitions
- Level 1 evidence
- Level 2 interpretation
- Level 3 comparisons
- Level 4 speculative frontier, if appropriate
- Level 5 unsupported claims and corrections
- source trail map
- claim register entries
- visual/plate suggestions
- reader reconstruction notes
