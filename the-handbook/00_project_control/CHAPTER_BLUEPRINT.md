# Chapter Blueprint

Use this blueprint for every Handbook chapter.

```markdown
# Chapter NN — Title

## Chapter purpose

State what this chapter preserves, explains, or reconstructs.

## Reader promise

By the end of this chapter, the reader should be able to:

- understand the core topic
- identify the strongest evidence
- separate evidence from interpretation
- follow the source trail
- recognize unsupported claims

## Level 1 — Core Facts / Well-Supported

Use primary sources, archaeological records, artifact catalogs, reliable translations, and cautious scholarship.

## Level 2 — Interpretation / Scholarly and Contextual

Explain what can reasonably be inferred, where scholars disagree, and what remains uncertain.

## Level 3 — Connections / Comparative

Compare motifs, structures, systems, and recurring human problems across cultures. Clearly label comparison as comparison.

## Level 4 — Speculative Frontier

Explore possibilities only when useful and clearly marked speculative.

## Level 5 — Unsupported Claims / Corrections

List common false or exaggerated claims and explain why they do not currently hold.

## Source Trail

| Claim | Source type | Source | Confidence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Claim Register Entries

| ID | Claim | Level | Status | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Visual / Plate Plan

- plate idea
- diagram idea
- archive facsimile idea
- map/timeline/table idea

## Continuity Note

Explain why this chapter would still be useful if the reader had to rebuild knowledge from fragments.
```
